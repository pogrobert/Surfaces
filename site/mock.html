Surfaces

Pictures for every page of your site, drawn from one colour. Post covers, link previews, headers. No image model, no API key.

Usage

One brand, made once. Every picture after it follows from its title.

A post's cover
<Cover brand={brand} title={post.title} />
A link preview
toJpeg(brand.og({ title, drawTitle: true }))
A profile header
npx @pogrobert/surfaces kit

Questions

Does it call an AI model?
No. The pictures are drawn by code, on your machine or your server. The same title gives the same bytes on every build.
Whose font are the titles in?
Yours. A title is set by your page over the place the art keeps for it, or drawn into an image file from your own font file.
What does it cost?
Nothing. MIT No Attribution: use it for anything, with no credit needed.
